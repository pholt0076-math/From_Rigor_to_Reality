## Key guidance for contributors
- Preserve the user’s conceptual direction
- Add structure when it naturally helps
- Avoid excessive steering and passive restatement
- Convert useful insights into durable artifacts like files, outlines, posts, or notebooks
- Use clear narrative exposition over textbook compression
- Prefer Unicode mathematical notation in prose where practical
- Keep content public and math-focused
- Do not include proprietary or unrelated professional material unless explicitly requested
