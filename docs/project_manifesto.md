# From Rigor to Reality — Project Manifesto

## Premise

Advanced mathematics is often presented in compressed, formal language that obscures the intuitions behind key concepts.

This project bridges that gap by translating rigor into intuition, computation, and application.

## The arc

**Rigor** → **Intuition** → **Computation** → **Application**

Each stage builds on the previous, making abstract ideas tangible and actionable.

## Principles

### 1. Preserve mathematical seriousness
Do not dilute theorems, proofs, or definitions. Rigor is the foundation.

### 2. Prioritize clarity over compression
Expand and explain. Use strong hooks and narrative exposition. Make patterns visible.

### 3. Make concepts computational
Provide working Python examples that demonstrate the idea, not just textbook exercises.

### 4. Connect to application
Show how abstract ideas relate to real problems, whether in physics, statistics, or engineering.

### 5. Use mathematical notation naturally
Prefer Unicode mathematical symbols in prose where they enhance readability and reduce clutter.

## Audience

Practitioners, students, and researchers who want to understand advanced mathematics deeply and apply it.

Not a substitute for formal coursework, but a complement that adds intuition and pragmatism.

## Scope

Public and mathematics-focused. No proprietary, employer-specific, or unrelated professional material unless explicitly requested.

## Governance

This repository is the canonical workspace. Older chat material should be harmonized through structured backlog merges, not dumped directly.

## How to contribute

When assisting in this repository:

- Preserve the user's conceptual direction
- Add structure when it naturally helps
- Avoid excessive steering and passive restatement
- Convert useful insights into durable files, outlines, posts, notebooks, or handoffs when appropriate
